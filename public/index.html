ShowSound
This app captures live sound through your mic, applies real-time EQ and a hearing-safe dB limiter, and plays it through your earbuds — like a smart hearing monitor for live shows.
You'll see a mic permission prompt. Audio is processed entirely on your device — nothing is recorded or sent anywhere.
⚠️ Put your earbuds in before tapping Start. Keep volume at a comfortable level first.
🎸
Show Mode On
ShowSound will keep your screen awake so audio doesn't stop during your show.

For best results: set Auto-Lock to Never in iPhone Settings → Display & Brightness → Auto-Lock.

⚠️ Note: pressing the side button to manually lock your phone will still stop audio. Keep your screen on during the show.
